The oven knob is the AI model's temperature. When the model writes, it picks one word after another.

Low temperature (1 = 0.2): it almost always picks the most likely word, so Oma is strict and predictable. Ask the same thing twice and you get nearly the same answer.

High temperature (3 = 1.4): it takes more creative and random choices, so Oma rambles and surprises you.

Her instructions never change. The knob only changes the temperature.
